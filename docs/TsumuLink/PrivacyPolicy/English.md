**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Tsumu Link app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Koichiro Ueki (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

**Your Links Stay With You**

The links you save in the Application, and their titles, site names and images, are stored only on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. The Service Provider cannot access them and never receives them. The Application does not require an account.

To show a title and image for a saved link, the Application downloads that web page directly from its website, in the same way a web browser does. The website may see your device's IP address, as it would when you visit it in a browser.

**Information Collection and Use**

To understand how the Application is used and to fix problems, the Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They may collect:

*   Which screens of the Application you view and which features you use (for example, opening a link or swiping a card), without the links or their titles
*   An identifier for this installation of the Application (not the advertising identifier)
*   Your approximate region, derived from your device's IP address
*   Crash reports and diagnostic information, such as your device model and operating system version

This information is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. The Application does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and does not show advertisements.

**Third Party Access**

The Application uses third-party services that have their own Privacy Policy about handling data:

*   [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
*   [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)
*   iCloud (Apple), for syncing your links: [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

The Service Provider may disclose the information described above:

*   as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
*   when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it. You can delete your links at any time in the Application (move them to the Trash and empty it). Links synced to iCloud can also be deleted in the Settings app (your name > iCloud).

**Data Retention Policy**

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google according to the Firebase retention settings. Your links are kept on your device and in your iCloud until you delete them; links in the Trash are deleted automatically after the period you choose in the Application.

**Children**

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

**Changes**

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes by updating this page. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
